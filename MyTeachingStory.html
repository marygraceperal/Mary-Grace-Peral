A Journey of Adaptability and Professional Growth

Resilience and Pedagogical Flexibility in Education

My professional journey since graduating from Father Saturnino Urios University in 2009 has been defined by adaptability and a commitment to meeting the "exigency of service" under diverse and challenging circumstances.

I began my career trained in the traditional structured approach (Review, Motivation, Discussion, Application). At that time, the RBEC Curriculum was in place, where I taught Mathematics in a linear, subject-specific format (Algebra, Geometry, and Trigonometry). However, my transition to the K-12 Curriculum in 2015 marked a significant shift in my pedagogy. I embraced the 4A’s model (Activity, Analysis, Abstraction, and Application) and was honored to be selected as a Demonstration Teacher, modeling these new student-centered strategies for my colleagues.

My experience in both private and public sectors—specifically in "far-flung" integrated schools—forced me to become a versatile educator. I have successfully taught outside my specialization in subjects such as Science, Social Science (Araling Panlipunan), and MAPEH. This experience taught me how to quickly master new content and deliver it effectively to learners of all levels, from Primary to Tertiary.

In 2014, I was appointed as a Bookkeeper Designate at Butuan City Comprehensive School. Despite having no formal background in accountancy, I dedicated myself to mastering school financial systems through DepEd training and self-study. This role not only sharpened my numeracy skills but also built a foundation that allowed me to teach Fundamentals of Accountancy, Business, and Management (FABM) to Senior High School students at Pareja Integrated School starting in 2017.

The COVID-19 pandemic was the ultimate test of my flexibility. I successfully managed the transition from traditional classroom instruction to Blended Learning, balancing the physical distribution of printed modules with the delivery of lessons via video conferencing. I adapted to the MELCs (Most Essential Learning Competencies) framework, ensuring that quality education continued despite the limitations of the era.

Today, as we navigate the post-pandemic landscape, the ARAL program initiatives, and the rapid rise of the artificial intelligence era, I remain a steadfast advocate for continuous learning. I dedicate myself to implementing innovative classroom strategies that harness emerging technologies, ensuring my students stay meaningfully connected to modern digital trends. My career has proven that I am not just a Mathematics specialist, but a resilient, forward-thinking professional ready to embrace any technological or curricular evolution to ensure student success.
