Simple Interest (Future Value)
FV = P(1 + r · t)

When to use: Calculate the future balance of a lump sum earning linear interest strictly on the initial principal (P).

Interest: ₱3,000.00 | Future Value: ₱23,000.00
Real-World Example:
Depositing ₱20,000 (P = 20,000) at 5% simple annual interest (r = 0.05) for 3 years (t = 3):
FV = 20,000 × (1 + 0.05 × 3) = ₱23,000.00
